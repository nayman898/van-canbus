ESP32 cabin case V3 LOW PROFILE - half-size Adafruit Perma-Proto
All dimensions millimetres. Physical fit has NOT been verified.

PRINT FIRST
  board_fit_test.stl - rear screw, front corner retainers and board seating.
  screw_fit_test.stl - left to right: 2.3 / 2.5 / 2.7 mm blind pilots.
  Try the actual screws by hand. Default case pilot is 2.5 mm.
  Use the fit frame with the complete unpowered assembly and USB plug.
  If V2's fit frame already seated correctly, reuse it; bottom support and
  retainer geometry are unchanged. Owner confirmed the bottom offsets fit.
  The open centre does not check all pins: measure the longest underside
  projection separately; it must be shorter than 6 mm with clearance remaining.

CASE: print one each of base.stl, lid.stl, cable_panel.stl.
Print the V3 base and cable panel. An existing V2 lid can be reused unchanged.
V2's cable panel is too tall for V3; V1 parts do not fit this footprint.
Already oriented on the print bed. 100% scale, millimetres, no supports.
Start with 0.2 mm layers, four walls, 30-40% infill.
PETG for initial fitting; select a material rated for the measured mounting
location temperature before permanent installation. Avoid sun-exposed mounting.

FASTENERS (not included)
  4 x nominal 3 x 10 mm pan-head thread-forming screws FOR THERMOPLASTIC: lid.
  1 x nominal 3 x 6 mm pan-head thread-forming screw FOR THERMOPLASTIC: PCB rear.
  Machine screws require tapped holes; the default pilots are not tapped.
  Mounting ears: two 4.2 mm clearance holes, choose screws for mounting surface.
  Do not force screws or use a power driver. Match the pilot to the screw maker's
  guidance and coupon result; change screw_pilot in esp32_case.json if needed.

ASSEMBLY
  Owner photo PXL_20261016_124356420.jpg (October 16, 2026) confirms the USB
  points toward the cable end and covers the front proto-board mounting hole.
  No screw/post is used at that hidden hole.
  Mount board with USB toward the removable panel. Tilt the rear upward, slide
  the front PCB corners under the two fixed lips, lower the rear onto its boss,
  then fit the ONE accessible rear screw. Do not flex the PCB or force a fit.
  Each front contact zone is 2 mm wide and extends 1.2 mm into the board edge.
  These zones must be clear of components, solder and wires on both faces.
  Check particularly the wires near the right front corner shown in the photo.
  Nothing may touch underside pins/solder. Leave the rear screw pad clear.
  Slide panel into the front grooves, open notches facing up.
  Centred USB opening: 18 x 16 mm. CAN wire opening to its right: 6 x 16 mm.
  These openings admit insulated cables, not bare pins or a guaranteed plug type.
  Check the actual USB plug fits before fastening. There is 10 mm from the
  PCB front edge to the outer case face, less where the panel/guides sit.
  An already fixed ESP32 facing another direction may need a flexible USB
  extension or a revised layout. Never bend the board connector to align it.
  Secure cables externally near the case so pulls do not reach solder joints.
  Fit lid with its lip inside the walls; the open side of the lip faces cables.
  Tighten by hand just enough to seat it. Leave antenna area free of metal/wires.

DESIGN ENVELOPE
  PCB: 50.8 x 81.28 x 1.6; two holes 73.66 mm apart along centreline.
  Allowed below PCB: 6 mm, measured from floor top to PCB underside.
  Owner measured from PCB TOP to tallest CAN terminal: 13 mm.
  Allowed above PCB: 13 mm components + 3 mm headroom.
  Body including lid: 60.8 x 100.28 x 28.4; width with mounting ears: 76.8.
  Body footprint is 26.8% smaller than V1 (74.8 x 111.28).
  Side/rear PCB margins are 5 / 9 mm; front margin is 10 mm.
  Height reduced 6 mm from V2; bottom offsets remain unchanged at owner's
  request. Full assembly/lid fit still needs checking before tightening.
  Vented, unsealed cabin prototype. Not an underhood or waterproof case.
  No external SD opening or mount included; card module has not been selected
  or installed. Revise when its actual layout is known.
